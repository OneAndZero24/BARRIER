Shared setup (DDPM)

- Unlearning recipe (main code, ablation_regions.py, same as the paper's λ sweep):
  - Random-Label objective: airplane-conditioned predictions are pushed toward the "automobile" prediction.
  - Adam, lr 1e-4, 3000 steps, no remain-set loss (weight 0).
  - Protection: k=32, two-corner boxes at the 5th/95th percentiles, infinity scale 20.
- Metrics:
  - UA (unlearning accuracy): share of the 500 airplane-conditioned samples that a ResNet-34 classifier does not call airplane. Higher means more forgetting.
  - RA (remain accuracy): ResNet-34 accuracy on 4,500 samples conditioned on the 9 other classes (500 each).
  - FID: 45k generated samples of the 9 remaining classes (guidance 2.0); lower is better. The unchanged model is about 15.2.
  - The ResNet-34 resizes images to 224×224, same as the paper and the SEMU baseline.
- λ sweep baseline (summary.csv): 4 seeds per λ (0, 1, 2, 1234).
